Security Is Built In

Attendance data includes personal identifiers, schedules, and operational details. We design every layer of AttendPro with safeguards that reduce risk and keep teams in control.

Role-Based Access

Admin and manager roles are clearly separated. Sensitive actions such as edits, deletions, and payroll updates are protected and visible to authorized users only.

Token Protection

Encrypted tokens and secure sessions keep accounts protected. Sessions expire when the browser is closed to reduce the risk of unauthorized access on shared devices.

Safe Uploads

Profile uploads are validated before storage. This prevents accidental or unsafe file types from entering the system.

Operational Safeguards

Admin-only controls for updates and deletions.
Clear audit visibility for record changes.
Secure logout that clears active sessions.
Offline actions are queued and verified before sync.
Role separation for payroll and attendance workflows.

Your Data, Your Control

Responsible Defaults

Sensitive fields are protected by default, while everyday attendance actions remain quick and user friendly.

Clear Recovery Paths

Mistakes happen. The platform supports controlled edits by authorized users so historical records stay accurate.

Compliance Ready

Exportable data and consistent policy controls help teams prepare for compliance checks and internal audits.
